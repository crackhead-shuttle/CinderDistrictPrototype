# Vertical slice plan

## Deliverable

- one third-person player
- one compact city district
- one drivable car
- day/night mood lighting
- pause menu and settings
- traffic loop
- save system

## Phase 1

- basic ground, road, sidewalk, and building shell layout
- third-person movement and sprint
- jump and camera follow
- vehicle entry and exit

## Phase 2

- traffic loop and ambient city feel
- settings and quality presets
- save/load and pause menu

## Phase 3

- polish, lighting balance, and playability tuning
- optimize for browser performance
