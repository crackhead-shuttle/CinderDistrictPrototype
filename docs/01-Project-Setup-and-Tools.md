# Game design document

## Vision

Create an original open-world city prototype centered on a compact but believable district. The player moves through a modern block, interacts with a vehicle, and navigates the environment with a cinematic camera and responsive input.

## Core pillars

- Believable city readability over scale
- Strong visual atmosphere with realistic material response
- Responsive third-person movement and vehicle handling
- Modular and testable systems
- Strong vertical-slice first milestone

## Target gameplay loop

1. Enter the city district.
2. Walk, sprint, and navigate sidewalks and roads.
3. Approach vehicle, enter, and drive through the environment.
4. Observe traffic, lighting, and world ambience.
5. Access pause and settings menus.
6. Save state and continue testing.

## Key design principles

- Do not overbuild before the playable slice is proven.
- Prefer readable, modular architecture.
- Maximize quality in a compact district instead of building a large empty world.
- Keep systems testable and debuggable.
